Legacy prose recorded before actions were rows is shown read-only and is never on any form, so no save can change it. Each rating group and comment is named by its action's text (id "-text") so a screen reader can tell the rows apart. For anyone who can edit, the intro line is always shown: on a first meeting the card is otherwise empty, and an empty card read as a broken text box. With nothing to rate, the card says why — "first meeting" only on a new page with no held meeting at all (no meeting, no source; a new page is only ever shown to an editor); otherwise a general message that stays true for a saved first meeting reopened later (no source is kept for a saved meeting, so the template cannot tell the two apart). Read-only viewers get a neutral line, never an instruction to rate or add. "Not yet marked as held" is never offered as a reason here: an unheld previous meeting is being prepared, and starting a new one reopens it. Expects: carried_formset, meeting (None when new), source (new meetings only), can_edit. {% endcomment %}
