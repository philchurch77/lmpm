# Glossary

- **Report**: the member of staff a line meeting is about (shown on screen as "member of staff").
- **Line manager**: the person named as a report's current line manager; they complete and hold the meeting.
- **Line meeting**: one dated 1:1 meeting record between a report and their line manager.
- **Action**: one thing agreed at a line meeting, to be done before the next.
- **Carried-forward action**: an action agreed at the previous held meeting, reviewed at this one.
- **RAG rating**: an action's progress at review — Red = not done or blocked, Amber = in progress, Green = done.
- **Being prepared**: a line meeting not yet held; the report and line manager can both edit it.
- **Held**: a line meeting the line manager has marked as having taken place; read-only to the report.
- **Legacy action notes**: action text recorded as free prose before actions were rows; kept verbatim, read-only.
